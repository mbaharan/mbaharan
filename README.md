## About

I build ML systems that run on the edge — from clinical health sensing on Apple devices to scalable video intelligence on embedded GPUs. My work spans the full pipeline: signal processing, deep learning architecture design, on-device optimization, and real-world clinical validation. I hold a PhD in Electrical & Computer Engineering, a US patent in AI-powered health assessment, and have published 12+ papers across IEEE and ACM venues with 645+ citations.
